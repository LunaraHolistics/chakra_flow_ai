## Brand & Style

This design system embodies quiet luxury, clinical mindfulness, and editorial precision tailored for high-end holistic practitioners, certified energy therapists, and integrative health clinicians. It deliberately rejects mystical clichés, neon psychedelia, and generic consumer wellness tropes in favor of an authoritative diagnostic environment that mirrors high-frequency clinical equipment, scientific observation, and haute horlogerie aesthetics.

The design movement combines **Tonal Minimalist Dark Mode** with **Refined Editorial Skeuomorphism**—using deep atmospheric plum-tinted dark spaces, razor-thin luminous hairline borders, and subdued spectral data visualization. The interface feels weightless, focused, and meditative, transforming complex energetic diagnostics into structured, actionable clinical narratives.

The palette operates on calibrated luminescence against deep, light-absorbing plum-charcoal substrates.

## Typography

The typographic tension pairs the editorial, humanistic grace of **Playfair Display** with the clinical crispness of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Display & Large Numerals:** Use Playfair Display for clinical summary titles, energetic equilibrium indices, and primary percentage scores. Numerical figures rendered in Playfair Display convey clinical luxury akin to fine diagnostic instruments.
- **Micro-Labels & Diagnostics:** Use Plus Jakarta Sans in semi-bold uppercase (`label-caps`) with elevated tracking for technical parameters, chakra status tags, frequency hertz markers, and clinical organ mapping.
- **Body & Clinical Notes:** Use Plus Jakarta Sans regular for clinical documentation, observation logs, and therapeutic prescription narratives to ensure effortless mobile legibility.

## Layout & Spacing

The mobile layout operates on a dynamic 4-column fluid mobile grid anchored by generous vertical cadence to foster an unhurried, focused clinical workflow.

- **Mobile Viewport Optimization:** Standard horizontal gutters are set to `1rem`, with outer viewport margins resting at `1.25rem` (16px and 20px respectively). This maintains maximum canvas usage for data curves while isolating content from hardware bezels.
- **Rhythm & Safe Areas:** Vertical spacing adopts an 8pt baseline scale. Crucial clinical summaries and biometric cards use `space-lg` (24px) for inner padding, establishing an airy, breathing canvas.
- **Touch Reachability:** All critical touch targets are placed within the lower 60% of the display canvas (thumb zone), ensuring seamless one-handed operation during active clinical sessions.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use harsh drop shadows. Instead, it relies on **Luminous Surface Stacking**, **Fine Structural Borders**, and **Subtle Spectral Radial Halos**.

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - *Base:* `#0F0D15` with no shadow.
  - *Resting Cards:* `#1A1626` backed by a 1px border of `#2E2844`.
  - *Floating Modals / Selected Nodes:* `#221D32` with a 1px outer stroke of `rgba(167, 139, 250, 0.22)` and a soft ambient glow: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 13, 21, 0.8), 0 0 24px 0 rgba(167, 139, 250, 0.08)`.
- **Radial Glow Halos:** Chakra nodes and active scanning indicators employ diffuse, low-intensity background halos (e.g., `radial-gradient(circle, rgba(167, 139, 250, 0.15) 0%, rgba(20, 17, 29, 0) 70%)`) placed behind cards to signify active alignment states without cluttering UI borders.

## Shapes

The shape system employs generous, organic curvature paired with surgical border precision to balance warmth with clinical technology:
- **Base Cards & Surfaces:** Standard cards utilize `1.25rem` to `1.5rem` (rounded-2xl to rounded-3xl) to evoke organic serenity and physical stones.
- **Pills & Interactive Actions:** Buttons, operational badges, and status tokens utilize full pill shapes (`rounded-full` / `9999px`).
- **Data Meters & Progress Tracks:** Ultra-thin tracks with rounded capsules for track tips to prevent visual harshness.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Diagnostic Action:** Full pill (`min-height: 52px`), filled with `#A78BFA`, label in `#0F0D15` Plus Jakarta Sans Bold. Hover/active states exhibit an ethereal diffuse ring: `0 0 16px rgba(167, 139, 250, 0.35)`.
- **Secondary / Ghost Action:** Full pill with `#1A1626` background, 1px `#2E2844` border, and `#F9F8FC` typography. On press, borders light up to `#A78BFA`.
- **Touch Target Law:** All interactive nodes (e.g., chakra anatomical points, stepper buttons, timeline scrubbers) must occupy a minimum bounding touch target of 48x48px, regardless of the visual point diameter (which may be as delicate as 8px).

### Diagnostic Pill Badges
- **Format:** Compact, high-density status capsules using `label-caps` typography, 1px border, and translucent colored fills.
- **Balanced State (`Equilibrado`):** Background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`, border `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)`, text `#10B981`. Accompanied by a 4px solid pulsing emerald dot.
- **Attention Required (`Precisa de atenção`):** Background `rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.12)`, border `rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.35)`, text `#F97316`.

### Cards & Clinical Diagnostic Panels
- **Structure:** Encased in `#1A1626` with a crisp 1px border of `#2E2844`. Padding is consistently 20px or 24px (`space-lg`).
- **Chakra Overview Card:** Houses the Playfair Display Roman numeral/chakra Sanskrit title, biological indicator badge, a horizontal breathing bar, and percentage value in Playfair metric typography.

### Ultra-Thin Breathing Gauges & Metric Meters
- **Track:** Height of 3px to 4px, background `rgba(46, 40, 68, 0.6)`.
- **Fill:** Uses the specific chakra's clinical data token with a soft leading-edge glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 8px [ChakraColor]`).
- **Animation Motion:** When visualizing active balance, gauges feature subtle cyclic easing (2.5s duration) mimicking human respiratory rhythm.

### Inputs & Clinical Notation Fields
- **Container:** Dark field (`#14111D`), 1px border (`#2E2844`), 14px interior padding, rounded-xl.
- **Focus State:** Border transitions gracefully to `#A78BFA` with an ambient glow of `0 0 12px rgba(167, 139, 250, 0.2)`. Placeholder text remains strictly subdued in `#655F78`.
